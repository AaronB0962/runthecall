Run the Call

Run the Call is an AI-powered scenario trainer for EMT and paramedic students: realistic patients, a live cardiac monitor, 12- and 15-lead ECGs, and exam-style debriefs.

Training tool only. AI can be wrong. Nothing in the app is medical advice. Always follow your local protocols and medical direction.
