Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Ronald Beach, doing business as Beach Applications · beachmadeapps@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password, which is stored only in hashed form (never in plain text).
  • Your calls: the text you type or speak during calls, your monitor actions, scores, debriefs, quiz results, saved and paused calls, and your progress. Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech service (Apple or Google); we receive only the text, never audio.
  • Reports and messages: reports you send about AI responses or grades (with that call's transcript), and your messages with the App's administrator.
  • Shared calls: calls you share or save (the scenario only), and your display name and score on a call's class leaderboard.
  • Protocols you upload: text, PDFs and photos of protocols or drug cards that you choose to add.
  • Subscription status: your plan, renewal dates and usage counts. Apple or Google handle payments; we never receive your card number.
  • Basic technical data: device type, app version and error logs, used to keep the App working.

Push Dose progress (drills, streaks, XP and drug-card scores) and 12-Lead Lab quiz progress are saved only on your device and are not sent to us.

How we use it

To run the App: create and grade your calls, keep your history, enforce plan limits, answer reports and messages, provide support, keep the service secure, and improve the App. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Our AI provider (Anthropic): call text, the scenario and your protocols are sent to generate patient responses, grades and quizzes, and to read protocol photos.
  • Hosting and database providers (Supabase) that store your account and data for us.
  • Apple or Google, which process your subscription payments under their own privacy policies, and RevenueCat, which confirms your subscription status with them using your account ID.
  • The App's administrator, who can read reports you send and your messages with them.
  • Other users, only for calls you share or save (the scenario) and leaderboards (your display name and score).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account (Account › Delete account), we delete your account data, including your messages, within 30 days, except limited records we must keep by law (such as purchase records). Calls you shared or saved to the class library and your leaderboard scores are deleted too, so those call codes stop working.

Your choices and rights

You can view and delete your data in the App, or email beachmadeapps@gmail.com to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example California or Washington), you may have additional rights to know, correct or delete your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

No patient information

Do not enter real patient information anywhere in the App, including calls, reports and messages. The App is for training with fictional patients and is not designed to store protected health information.

Age

The App is for people 16 and older, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit and hashed passwords. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

We will post updates here and ask you to review material changes in the App. Questions or requests: beachmadeapps@gmail.com.
