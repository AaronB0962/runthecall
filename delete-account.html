Delete your Run the Call account

Beach Applications · beachmadeapps@gmail.com

In the app (fastest)

Open Run the Call, tap your initials at the top right, then Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

By email

If you can't open the app, email beachmadeapps@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your account (name, email, password), plan and usage records, call history, scores, saved and paused calls, debriefs, uploaded protocols, reports you sent, and calls you shared with a class. This happens immediately in the app, and from backups within 30 days.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you, except where the law requires us to keep limited purchase records. Apple or Google keep their own records of your subscription purchases.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings (iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).
